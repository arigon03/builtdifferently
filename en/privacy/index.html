2. In short

This website uses no tracking or analytics tools, sets no cookies of its own, and loads no fonts from third parties. The only exception is the Digistore24 trust badge on the sales pages (see section 4). I do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

The website is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time, the page requested, and your browser, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

4. Purchase through Digistore24

Ordering, payment, and download are handled by Digistore24, which collects your order data (for example name, email address, country, and payment details) as an independent controller. The Digistore24 privacy policy applies. I receive the data needed for delivery and support (name, email address, country, order details). I never see payment data such as card numbers.

The sales pages include a Digistore24 trust badge at the bottom. It is loaded from digistore24.com, and Digistore24 processes technical data such as your IP address and may set cookies. The basis is my legitimate interest in making the purchase through Digistore24 transparent.

5. Contact by email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply. My mailbox is hosted by Gmail (Google), so data may be processed in the USA. I delete messages when they are no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep them.

6. Newsletter and marketing emails: how to unsubscribe

I do not send a newsletter at the moment. If I start one, it will be only with your explicit consent, and every email will contain an unsubscribe link.

You can opt out at any time and object to the use of your email address for marketing: click the unsubscribe link in the email, or send a short message to builtdifferently.book@gmail.com. Unsubscribing is free and takes effect immediately.

7. Instagram

This website links to Instagram but embeds nothing from it. Only when you click the link does Meta Platforms process data under its own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data and have it corrected, deleted, or restricted, object to its processing, and request a copy of it. Email builtdifferently.book@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or to the data protection authority in your country.
